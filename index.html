Yucatán Peninsula · Gulf of Mexico

Chicxulub

66.04 Ma · K–Pg boundarycrater 180–200 km

An asteroid about 10–15 km wide struck a shallow carbonate shelf here and ended the Cretaceous. The crater now lies under roughly a kilometre of younger limestone. At the surface, only a ring of cenotes and a trough a few metres deep trace its rim.
